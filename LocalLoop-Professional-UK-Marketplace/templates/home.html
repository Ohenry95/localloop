GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers.

Everything starts with finding a deal you love.

What is LocalLoop?＋

LocalLoop is a local-deals marketplace designed to help people discover offers, experiences and services from independent businesses around the UK.

How do local deals work?＋

Explore published offers, check the deal details and conditions, then follow the checkout process when purchases are enabled. A voucher should only be treated as confirmed once payment has been verified and the booking appears in your account.

Can my business join LocalLoop?＋

Yes. Business owners can create a partner profile and submit offers for review. Offers are reviewed before publication, and payment payouts require the relevant Stripe onboarding and verification.

Are all deals available everywhere in the UK?＋

Deals are location-specific. Search by town or postcode to find offers in the area you want to explore. Availability and redemption conditions are set by each participating business.
